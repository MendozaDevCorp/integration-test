Why the page is this tall

The tracker emits scroll_depth at 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%. A short page reports 100% immediately on load, because there is nothing to scroll, which looks like success while actually testing nothing. The filler below exists so the thresholds fire in sequence, the way they would on a real landing page.

Deploy correlation

Every release is annotated on the timeline with its author, diff size, and rollout window, so regressions can be traced without cross-referencing four tools.

Ownership routing

Alerts follow the service catalogue rather than a static on-call list, which keeps unrelated teams out of incident channels they cannot act on.

Signal retention

Raw events stay queryable for ninety days and rolled-up metrics stay indefinitely, so quarter-over-quarter comparisons survive a retention window.

Import paths

Backfill from existing providers so the timeline is useful on day one instead of after a quarter of accumulated history.

Access control

Scoped tokens per environment, with read-only defaults, so a compromised integration cannot mutate production configuration.

Audit trail

Every configuration change records actor, timestamp, and previous value, which turns "who turned this off" into a query rather than an investigation.
